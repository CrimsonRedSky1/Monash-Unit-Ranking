What we import

Records may include unit code, title, faculty or school, description, credits, teaching year and source URL. Availability depends on the source. We organise these records so students can browse and compare units; ratings and review text are contributed separately.

Coverage and freshness

The catalogue is not a complete list of every university or every unit. Coverage notices identify partial imports. Imports are not currently refreshed automatically, and a successful import does not guarantee that a unit is still offered. Check the linked official source before making study decisions.

Names, logos and ownership

University names, logos and source material belong to their respective owners. Their use here identifies the institution and does not imply affiliation or endorsement. Monash and RMIT logo files were sourced from their websites; Melbourne and Deakin logo files were sourced from Wikimedia. No ownership of university branding or handbook text is claimed.

Corrections and rights enquiries

Email LunchTable@gmail.com with the university, unit code, affected page and official source where possible. Please also use this address for attribution or removal concerns.
