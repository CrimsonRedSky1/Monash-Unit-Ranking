Privacy policy

Prototype notice · Updated 31 October 2026. Accounts and community features are available when this website runs with the LunchTable server. GitHub Pages remains a static preview.

Account information

We store your chosen username, university, enrolment year, current year of study, semester, optional bio and account creation time. These profile details are public. We store a salted password hash, not your plain-text password. This version does not collect an email address during sign-up and does not verify university enrolment.

Reviews, recommendations and connections

Signed-in reviews include your account username, unit, rating, year taken, teaching period, text and creation time. Recommendations and follower/following connections are public, including through the public API and community feed. Reviews contribute to ratings and rankings. Legacy reviews remain under their original display names and are not automatically assigned to accounts. Do not include student IDs, contact details or private information about others.

Where information is stored

In server mode, account and social information is stored in the operator’s SQLite database. Your browser holds an essential sign-in cookie; the server stores a hash of the session token. Sessions expire after seven days and sign-out invalidates the current session. Bookmarks remain in browser storage and are not synced to your account. In the static preview, reviews are browser-local and cannot be published or linked to shared profiles.

Logs and service providers

The development server logs request paths, times and client addresses to its console, and temporarily counts authentication attempts by client address to limit repeated attempts. It does not log passwords. This prototype adds no advertising or analytics service. A future hosting provider may process operational logs; provider names, locations and retention arrangements will be disclosed before public deployment.

Retention and your choices

Accounts, reviews and connections currently remain until manually removed by the operator; there is no automatic account expiry. You can edit your study details and bio, unfollow profiles, and remove your recommendations. Self-service account and review deletion, password recovery and email verification are not yet implemented. Clearing browser storage does not delete server data. Browser-local reviews and bookmarks can be removed by clearing this site’s browser storage.

Contact and requests

Contact LunchTable@gmail.com for access, corrections, deletion requests or privacy questions. Include your username and enough context to identify the record, but never send passwords or identity documents. The operator’s legal identity, hosting arrangements and retention procedures still need to be finalised before public launch.
