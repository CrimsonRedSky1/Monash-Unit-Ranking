About LunchTable

LunchTable is a place for students to share experiences and discover units they might enjoy.

Explore, compare and save

Browse university catalogues, compare review-based rankings and save units for later. The directory is designed to grow, although current coverage is limited to the imported universities and units.

Independent by design

LunchTable is not run by or affiliated with the universities listed. Reviews are personal experiences and should be read alongside official information.

Student accounts are planned

We want students to be able to sign up and review under a chosen account name. There is no sign-up or login yet. Current review forms use a display name; they do not verify student status or establish an account.

Questions or feedback? Contact LunchTable@gmail.com.
