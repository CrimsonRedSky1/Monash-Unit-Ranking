Privacy policy

Current prototype notice · Updated 30 October 2026. Student accounts and sign-up are not available yet. This notice describes the existing website; account-specific practices will be published before sign-up launches.

Information you provide

A review contains your chosen display name, unit, one-to-five-star rating, year and teaching period, review text and creation time. You do not need to enter your legal name. Avoid including contact details, student IDs, grades or information about other people.

Where reviews and bookmarks are stored

On the static website, reviews and bookmarks are stored in your browser’s local storage on this device. They are not automatically shared with other visitors or synced to another device. If a review server is connected, the form identifies this: submitted reviews are stored on that server and available to other visitors through its review API. A display name is not a verified account.

Reviews are used to display student experiences and calculate ratings and rankings. Bookmarks let you keep a shortlist. Fictional demo reviews are labelled and generated for testing; they are not saved as student submissions.

Hosting and external services

Loading the website sends ordinary web-request information, such as your IP address and browser information, to the hosting provider. The site currently loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives requests when fonts are fetched. Following handbook links takes you to university websites with their own privacy practices. The current application does not include advertising or analytics scripts.

Storage duration and your choices

Browser data remains until you clear this site’s storage or remove a bookmark. Clearing site data removes local reviews and bookmarks and cannot be undone. Shared server reviews do not currently have an automatic expiry or self-service edit/delete feature. Do not assume that clearing your browser removes a server submission.

For questions, access, correction or deletion requests involving shared reviews, contact LunchTable@gmail.com. Include the unit, display name and approximate submission date, but do not email passwords or student identity documents. We may need enough information to identify the relevant submission. This prototype does not yet offer verified account ownership.

Before student accounts launch

The policy will need to identify the service operator, account data collected, authentication provider, hosting locations, retention periods, deletion process and any sharing or overseas processing. We have not implemented or confirmed those arrangements. A chosen public account name should not be mistaken for anonymity: the service may need private account information to support sign-in.

Questions or concerns

Contact LunchTable@gmail.com for privacy questions or complaints. The operator’s legal identity is still to be confirmed before account launch.
