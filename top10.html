How rankings work

We balance average rating with review volume: score = (total stars + 5 × overall average) ÷ (review count + 5). The overall average uses all valid, non-demo reviews of units in our catalogue, and stays the same when you choose a university. This tempers small samples; it does not guarantee review authenticity.

Ties are broken by the number of five-star ratings, then total review count, then unit ID. Only units with reviews are included. All years and teaching periods count equally. Rankings cover imported units only; some university catalogues are incomplete.
