Terms of service

Prototype terms · Updated 30 October 2026. These terms describe current use. Account-specific terms will be provided before registration becomes available.

What LunchTable provides

LunchTable helps students browse imported university unit information, save units and share experiences. It is an independent service, not an official university handbook. Ratings reflect available reviews, not academic quality guarantees or university endorsement.

Using reviews responsibly

Only submit experiences you can honestly describe. Use a display name you are entitled to use; do not impersonate others, manipulate rankings or submit spam. Follow the review guidelines. You retain rights in your original review and permit LunchTable to store and display it as described in the review form and privacy policy.

University information

Imported information can be incomplete or out of date. Check the official handbook for prerequisites, availability, credit points and enrolment decisions. University names and logos identify institutions and remain the property of their respective owners.

Availability and reports

This prototype may change or be unavailable. Bookmarks and browser-only reviews may be lost if browser storage is cleared. Report inaccurate data, inappropriate content or rights concerns to LunchTable@gmail.com. Automated moderation, verified accounts and self-service review deletion are not yet available.

Your rights and future changes

Nothing on this page is intended to exclude rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. We will update this page as functionality changes. These prototype terms require final operator details and review before accounts or a public shared-review service launch.
