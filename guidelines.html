Review guidelines

Share your own experience

Describe a unit you took, give an honest enjoyment rating, and include the year and teaching period. Explain what helped you learn, what was challenging and what future students should know.

Be constructive and protect privacy

Discuss teaching and learning experiences without personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language or threats. Do not post private contact details, student IDs, assessment answers or other people’s personal information.

Keep ratings trustworthy

Do not impersonate another person, post fabricated reviews, advertise or coordinate ratings to manipulate the leaderboard. Fictional test reviews must remain clearly labelled as demos.

Report a concern

Email LunchTable@gmail.com with the unit and review details. Do not repeat unnecessary private information in your report. Reports are handled by contact email; in-app reporting and automated moderation are not implemented yet.
